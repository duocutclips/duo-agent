# Morning brief

Keep it under 15 lines:
1. Today's calendar and the one thing that must get done.
2. Emails that need a reply today (from clients, leads, platforms, Whop). Link each; do not reply.
3. Approval queue: how many clips, outreach drafts and invoices are waiting in `queue/`.
4. Numbers since yesterday: new clip views and approved payouts from `data/clip-log.csv`, new leads.
5. The next step on this week's plan for this partner's 7 hours.
